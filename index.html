How the calculations work

Financing

Flat-rate payment = loan × (1 + annual rate × years) ÷ months. For reducing balance, payment = P × r ÷ [1 − (1 + r)−n], with end-of-month payments and a constant rate.

For an annual nominal rate, r = annual rate ÷ 12. For an effective annual rate, r = (1 + annual rate)1/12 − 1. Match the method and rate convention to your lender’s quote; the same percentage under different methods gives different payments. Rounding or lender fees can change the final instalment.

Running costs & insurance

Fuel litres = km ÷ km/L, or km × L/100 km ÷ 100. Fuel cost = litres × RM/L. Annual insurance, road tax and maintenance are divided by 12 as monthly sinking funds.

With a final insurance quote, NCD is already included. With a base premium, insurance = base × (1 − NCD%) + non-discounted costs. Enter taxes, stamp duty and add-ons in that last field at their actual quoted amounts. NCD applies only to the base premium.

Affordability & scope

Salary share = total monthly cost ÷ net salary. Your budget = net salary × chosen limit. Required salary = total monthly cost ÷ chosen limit.

This is a recurring cash-flow comparison. It excludes depreciation, resale value, inflation, early settlement and one-off purchase charges. Salary left is before housing, food, other commitments and savings. Road tax, fuel prices and all example values are editable inputs, not live quotes.
